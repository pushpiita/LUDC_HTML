About Leading University Debating Club

The Leading University Debating Club (LUDC) is a student-run organization founded to cultivate a spirit of intellectual discourse, persuasive speaking, and critical thinking among students. Since its inception, the club has been a breeding ground for aspiring debaters who represent the university nationally and internationally.

We organize weekly debate workshops, inter-departmental competitions, and participate in national debate championships. LUDC is committed to creating a community of thinkers and speakers who challenge norms and lead with their voices.
