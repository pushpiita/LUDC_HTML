About Our Club

The Leading University Debating Club (LUDC) is one of the most vibrant and intellectually dynamic student organizations on campus. Founded with the aim of nurturing confident speakers and critical thinkers, LUDC provides a platform for students to engage in meaningful discourse, express their views, and challenge perspectives.
We believe that debate is not just a competition of words, but a journey toward enlightenment, empathy, and empowerment. Through regular workshops, practice sessions, and competitive events, we help members sharpen their public speaking, logical reasoning, and argumentation skills.
LUDC proudly represents Leading University in inter-university and national debating tournaments across Bangladesh. Our members have gone on to earn accolades and play active roles in leadership, academia, and civic engagement.
But we are more than a club—we are a community. A community that thrives on mutual respect, shared learning, and the courage to voice the truth. Whether you're a seasoned debater or a curious beginner, LUDC welcomes you to join our mission: to lead with ideas, inspire with speech, and grow with every debate.
